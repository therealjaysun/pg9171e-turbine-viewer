# Direct Video Comparison: Combustion

Reviewed 2026-10-06 by opening and pausing the supplied YouTube video. This is a fresh visual comparison after R02, not a restatement of the geometric test results.

## Observed Frames

- [12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4r1-IMMS73s&t=741s): the cutaway exposes a ribbed forward liner, a substantially smooth aft liner, and a perforated flow sleeve whose aft end tapers inward. R02 had cooling lips continuing into the aft liner and a solid, untapered sleeve in this region.
- [12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4r1-IMMS73s&t=776s): the liner outlet has a circumferential row of narrow axial spring-seal fingers. R02 represented this as a continuous solid ring.
- [13:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4r1-IMMS73s&t=826s): the longitudinal section shows a short annular V-profile Venturi and separate central secondary-nozzle passage. R02's Venturi was too elongated and its profile did not close at its forward attachment.
- [14:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4r1-IMMS73s&t=841s): a dilution opening is visible in the smooth aft liner. The on-screen annotation/narration identifies three per liner; the single frame alone does not prove that population.
- [20:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4r1-IMMS73s&t=1245s): a transition-piece cutaway shows an aft cooling/vent plate and a pinned support bracket. The open round-to-sector duct agrees in general arrangement, but those plate perforations and the bracket machining remain visibly simplified in the reconstruction.

## Corrections

The aft liner is now smooth apart from its three dilution-port rims and outlet seal. Cooling lips are limited to the forward region. The flow sleeve has genuine air-admission openings and an inward-tapered aft wall. The outlet has separate narrow axial seal fingers. The shorter Venturi has a closed wall profile with a clear throat.

The sleeve's four rows of twelve holes and the seal's 96 fingers are illustrative populations, not counts recovered from the video. Their placement, diameters, thicknesses and exact taper remain inferred. Forward cooling-ring pitch, film-hole patterns, metering populations, impingement details and the center-nozzle geometry are still simplified. These changes improve specific visible discrepancies; they do not establish a difference-free replica.

## Regression Checks

All fourteen sleeves are ray-tested through the new air-admission openings, with adjacent intact-wall controls. Each liner is checked for an unobstructed smooth aft envelope, an open Venturi throat and retained throat wall. Existing cap, crossfire, dilution, metering, film-slot and transition-centerline checks remain active.
