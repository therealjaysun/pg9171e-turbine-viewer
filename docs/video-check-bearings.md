## Verification Boundary

The model is closer to the visible source in these named features, but it is not visually identical to the source animation. Running gaps remain deliberately enlarged. The base frame and trunnion supports are inferred and were not established by these bearing close-ups. No new claims about foundation geometry, shaft manufacture, coupling construction, oil-film thickness, or exact pad population follow from these frames.

The mechanical regression checks require split flanges to remain within the existing frame aperture, retain a clear feed path through those flanges, preserve all journal/shaft and thrust-face gaps, and keep the new lower casting drain open. They also check the two leveling-plate tiers and five rear pivot heads. These checks establish the modeled interfaces, not OEM dimensional accuracy.
